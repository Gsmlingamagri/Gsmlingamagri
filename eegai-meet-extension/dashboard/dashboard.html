Welcome to Eegai G Meet

Auto admit & attendance tracker for Google Meet — completely free.

  1. Sign in with Google — a Google Sheet called Eegai G Meet Attendance is created in your Drive.
  2. Join a meeting — attendance (join time, leave time and duration) is recorded automatically.
  3. Turn on Auto Admit from the floating Eegai panel in Meet to let people in without clicking.
  4. When the meeting ends the report opens, the CSV downloads and the rows are added to your Sheet.

A Google window opens — choose your Gmail account and click Allow once. No passwords are stored by Eegai.

Setup needed: the Google Sheet connector URL is empty. Open Settings and paste the Apps Script web app URL (see README).

Help Desk

How attendance is taken

Once you are inside a Google Meet call, Eegai records each participant's first join time, last leave time and total time in the call (rejoins are added up). When you leave the call or close the tab, the report is generated.

Google Sheet columns: Meeting Name | Meeting ID | Date | Participant | Email | In Time | Out Time | Duration | Attendance

Google Sheet connector

Apps Script web app URL used to save attendance to Google Sheets. Leave empty to use the built-in one.

Auto Admit

Inside a meeting, click the Auto Admit button in the green Eegai panel (top-right). When it is ON, anyone who asks to join is let in automatically. You must be the host or a co-host for Meet to show the Admit button.

Choose Only people in my participant lists in Settings to admit only known people.

Emails and absentees

Google Meet does not show participants' email addresses. Eegai matches each participant's display name against your Participant Lists to fill in the Mail ID. Link a list to a meeting link (or set a default list) to mark people who never joined as Absent.

Download the sample CSV to see the upload format.

Google accounts

Click your email address at the top right to Switch account, Add another account or Sign out. After you sign out, you can pick a different Gmail account. Each account has its own attendance Sheet.

Tips

  • Use Google Meet in English for the most reliable detection.
  • Keep the People panel open in large meetings so everyone is counted.
  • Reports that could not be saved to the Sheet (for example when offline) are retried automatically; you can also click Sync pending to Sheet.
